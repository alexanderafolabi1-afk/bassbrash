Services Built Differently.

A curated set of specialist services addressing what the industry has long overlooked. Click to read about each.

I
Frequency DNA Report
A data-driven sonic profile — who listens to your music, where they are, and exactly where to take your sound next.
£75
per report

Submit your three strongest tracks. We return a full written report — your listener psychographic profile, optimal release timing, and a curated list of playlist curators and editorial blogs that align specifically with your sound. Strategic intelligence previously reserved for signed artists with full label backing.

II
Ghost Credit Certificate
A timestamped record of creative contribution — for every collaborator who deserves documentation of their work.
£49
per certificate

Ghost-writing exists in every genre and every country. We issue a formally witnessed, date-stamped certificate recording your specific contribution — melody, lyrics, production, arrangement — with all project details stored permanently. An evidential record that protects your creative legacy.

III
The Vault
A secure, date-stamped archive for your unreleased work. Your creative timeline. Protected.
£25
per year

Upload demos, rough voice memos and works-in-progress. Every upload is date-stamped and stored securely. Artists lose copyright disputes because they cannot prove when something was created. The Vault is that proof — for twenty-five pounds a year.

IV
Culture Bridge
A managed creative introduction between artists from different cultural and sonic worlds.
£85
per match

We match you with a carefully selected artist from a different genre or culture based on your sound, goals and influences. We manage the introduction and provide a creative brief. An Arabic producer and a UK vocalist. A Korean beatmaker and a Brazilian MC. What you build together is entirely yours — we just open the door.

V
Sonic Brand Kit
A complete audio identity for brands, creators and businesses who communicate through sound.
£150
per kit

A custom sonic logo, background music pack in five moods, intro and outro stings, and full commercial rights — forever. Podcasters, YouTubers, startups, agencies. The creator economy has fifty million producers of content — every one of them needs this and almost none of them have it yet.
